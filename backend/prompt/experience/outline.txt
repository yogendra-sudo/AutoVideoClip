# Core mission: practical value and operationality
Your task is to cover in full** all the important experiences and techniques contained in the input text, while maintaining the moderate refinement** and avoiding excessive refinement. The objective is to extract **8-15 core topics**, each corresponding to video content **3-8 minutes**.

# The extraction principle

1. Practical value priority principle
-** Operational priority**: priority is given to extracting experience sharing with clear operational steps and practical skills
-** Problem resolution orientation**: focus on experiences that can address specific problems and meet real needs
-** Avoiding talk**: Skipping purely theoretical discussions, focusing on replicable, practical experiences

2. Principle of empirical integrity
-** Methodological system**: ensure that each experience is shared with a complete methodological system (background-method-impact-care)
-** Enriched details**: focus on a description of experience with specific details, operational points
-** Results validation**: Experience sharing that highlights practical results, success stories

3. The user applicability principle
- ** The threshold is reasonable**: priority is given to extracting elements of experience appropriate to the level of capacity of the target audience
-** Clear scene**: focus on experience with clearly applied scenarios, conditions of use
-** Replicability**: highlighting an empirical approach that is universally applicable and easy to replicate

## 4. Value-level principle
-** Core skills**: Prioritization of critical skills, core methodology experience sharing
- ** advance techniques**: high-level skills focused on efficiency and impact optimization
-**Guide on pit avoidance**: lessons learned to highlight error prevention and risk avoidance

# Collapse and Split

# # Merge conditions (the following should be combined into one topic):
- Different aspects or levels of the same skills or methods
- Interconnected operational steps or processes
- Different solutions or techniques to the same problem
- Different applications in similar settings.

# # Disaggregation conditions (the following should be divided into different topics):
- Experience in different skill areas or applications
- A clear distinction between basic and advanced skills
- Different types of problem resolution
- Separation of theoretical learning from practical application
