# The extraction principle

#1. Say value first
- ** Core content**: Priority extraction of core narratives and critical analysis of original work
- ** Keys**: Focus on critical twists, orgasms and good clips

**Audience interest**: ensure that each topic has a clear value and attraction

## 2. Content integrity
- ** Complete **: Each topic should contain a complete paragraph or description unit
- ** Complete analysis**: analysis and evaluation of works should maintain their logical integrity
** Complete text**: Introduction, roll-out, summary should be treated as a complete module
-** Background integrity**: Key backgrounders and mattresses should remain complete

#3 Audience experience
- ** rhythm control**: Considering the viewer's rhythm and focus degrees
- **Infodensity**: Balancing information and view comfort
- ** Recreational Value**: For entertainment interpretation, focus on fun and entertainment effects
-** Value of education**: For educational interpretation, focus on knowledge transfer and learning values

## 4. Level of interpretation
** Mainline interpretation** : Core interpretation of the main content of the work
-** Detailed analysis**: In-depth analysis of key details, techniques, background
-** Personal view**: personal opinion, evaluation and recommendation of the interpreter
- ** Interactive element**: interaction with the audience, questions and answers, or leading thinking
