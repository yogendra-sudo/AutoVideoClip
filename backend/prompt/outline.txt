# The extraction principle

1. Precedence of importance
-** Core topic priority**: priority is given to extracting the most valuable and inspiring core views and discussions for the audience
-**Infodensity assessment**: selection of high-intensity, high-profile clips as independent words Topic
- ** Opening value recognition**: background presentations, theme previews, personal perspectives, etc. with special focus on the opening of the video
- ** Value of transitional content**: without losing sight of important points of view and connecting content when the topic is converted
-** Avoiding redundancies**: similar views or repetitive discussions should be combined into one topic

2. The principle of time balance (flexible implementation)
-** Target duration**: 3-12 minutes video content per topic (best 6-8 minutes)
- ** Maximum hour**: try to avoid a topic session of less than 2 minutes
- ** Maximum duration**: individual topics should not exceed 15 minutes, too long to split
- ** Cover priority**: if important content is short but independent, it can be retained as a stand-alone topic
-** Merger strategy**: priority is given to merging relevant elements, but not mandatory consolidation leads to gaps in coverage

3. Principles of structural integrity
- ** Natural topic transition**: each topic should be subject to a clear change of subject or perspective
- ** Logical integrity**: ensure that each topic contains a complete logic of discussion and that no abrupt breakpoints occur
-** Consistency**: There should be logical consistency between topics and avoiding rigid division

## 4. Content quality principles
- ** Value orientation**: priority is given to extracting content of practical value to the audience (perspectives, experiences, methods, etc.)
-** Avoiding invalid content**: Skipping low-value content such as pure cold, oral, meaningless repetition
-** Stay focused**: each topic should have a clear, single core theme

# Analysis process (must be implemented)

Before extracting the outline, please analyse the system according to the following steps:

1. ** Full-text time-scan**: quick browsing of the entire text, identifying the main time nodes and subject points
2. ** Preliminary analysis**: focus on the first 10-15 minutes of analysis, identification of opening views, background presentations, etc.
3. **Synthesis of the paragraph**: analysis of the core discussion and main points of view in the intermediate part
4.** End-of-service examination**: analysis of final 5-10 minutes summary, additional views, etc.
5. ** Cover validation**: confirmation that each time period has corresponding topic coverage
6.** Topical integration**: reasonable integration based on relevance of content and long-term requirements
