# Core mission: commercial value and practicality
Your task is to cover all important business topics in the input text in their entirety** while maintaining the moderate refinement** and avoiding excessive refinement. The objective is to extract **8-15 core topics**, each corresponding to video content **3-8 minutes**.

# The extraction principle

1. Priority principle of commercial value
- **Performance priority**: priority is given to extracting the most valuable business views and analyses for investors, entrepreneurs, and workers
-**Data driver**: focus on content containing specific data, case analysis, market trends
-** Avoiding talk**: Skipping purely theoretical discussions, focusing on operational business strategies and practical experiences

2. The principle of time-sensitiveness
- ** hot topic**: priority is given to extracting current market hot spots, policy changes, industry dynamics
-**Trend analysis**: focus on projections and analysis of future business trends
-** Timing**: highlighting time-bound elements such as investment timing, business opportunity windows

3. Logical structure principles
-** Causation**: ensure that each topic contains a complete business logic chain (cause-process-result)
- **Risk return**: balanced discussion of business opportunities and potential risks
-**Multidimensional analysis**: covering various dimensions such as macroeconomics, industrial analysis, corporate fundamentals, etc.

## 4. Targeted audience principle
-** Layer Value**: Distinguishing between content appropriate for different levels of audiences (new entry vs professional step)
-** hands-on guidance**: priority is given to extracting what contains specific operational recommendations, tools recommendations
- ** Case rich**: focus on real business cases, sharing of success stories

# Collapse and Split

# # Merge conditions (the following should be combined into one topic):
- Discussion of different perspectives of the same company/industry
- Relevant investment strategy or business model discussions
- Multidimensional interpretation of the same economic phenomenon
- Continuous market analysis and forecasting

# # Disaggregation conditions (the following should be divided into different topics):
- Moving from macroeconomic to firm-specific analysis
- Independent discussions in different industries or markets
- Separate discussion of investment strategy and risk management
- A clear distinction between theoretical analysis and practical advice
