# Entertainment and variety-show timestamp localization prompt

For every topic in an entertainment video, locate its exact `start_time` and `end_time`.

## Core principles
1. **Complete entertainment:** Preserve a complete entertaining experience; do not ruin jokes or memorable moments.
2. **Natural pacing:** Follow the content's natural rhythm for a smooth and enjoyable viewing experience.
3. **Preserve the payoff:** Keep humor, guest chemistry, and the entertainment atmosphere intact.

## Determine `start_time`
Choose the natural start of the clip:
1. Topic introduction: the host or guest begins a new subject.
2. Game start: the rules are explained and the game officially begins.
3. Interaction start: a new exchange between guests begins.
4. Performance start: the performance or demonstration begins.
5. Scene transition: the segment or atmosphere clearly changes.

Retain necessary setup and atmosphere, the full setup of a joke, natural interaction, and the rhythm of the entertainment.

## Determine `end_time`
Choose the natural end:
1. The discussion ends before moving to another topic.
2. A game concludes and its result is revealed or summarized.
3. A complete round of interaction or conversation ends.
4. A performance or demonstration finishes.
5. The joke has fully landed.

Retain reactions after a joke, natural endings to interactions, emotional resolution, and audience or guest reactions.

## Errors to avoid
- Cutting off during a joke's payoff.
- Ending a game at a critical moment.
- Cutting off a guest's emotional expression.
- Interrupting the most engaging part of an interaction.
- Omitting audience reactions or the live atmosphere.
- Disrupting the natural pace.

## Duration guidance
- Standard duration: **3–8 minutes**.
- Light entertainment: 3–5 minutes for everyday humor or simple interaction.
- Substantive entertainment: 5–7 minutes for thoughtful humor or complex games.
- Strong performances: 6–8 minutes for talent showcases or important interviews.
- Adjustments: a compelling, complex game may extend to 10 minutes; a single joke or short exchange may be 2–3 minutes; an important in-depth interview may extend to 12 minutes.

## Preserve complete units
1. A joke's setup, payoff, and reaction.
2. A game's rules, play, and result.
3. The full conversation and interaction among guests.
4. The performance and audience response.
5. The complete emotional arc of a moving or warm moment.

## Input format
You will receive a JSON object containing an outline and SRT text:
```json
{
  "outline": [
    {
      "title": "Hilarious moments from a variety-show game",
      "subtopics": [
        "Funny misunderstandings while the rules are explained",
        "The celebrities' charming reactions to failed challenges",
        "Teammates' coordination and mistakes",
        "An unexpected reversal in the outcome"
      ]
    }
  ],
  "srt_content": "Complete SRT subtitle text"
}
```

## Output format
Return a JSON array and rename fields:
- `title` → `outline`
- `subtopics` → `content`
- Add `start_time` and `end_time`.

```json
[
  {
    "outline": "Hilarious moments from a variety-show game",
    "content": [
      "Funny misunderstandings while the rules are explained",
      "The celebrities' charming reactions to failed challenges",
      "Teammates' coordination and mistakes",
      "An unexpected reversal in the outcome"
    ],
    "start_time": "00:05:23,450",
    "end_time": "00:11:45,230"
  }
]
```

### Timestamp format
Use `HH:MM:SS,mmm` (hours:minutes:seconds,milliseconds). Milliseconds must contain exactly three digits.

## Timing examples

### Game segment
```text
Timeline:
00:05:20 - Host: "Next, let's play a game."
00:05:23 - Host: "Here are the rules..."
00:11:42 - Guest: "That was hilarious!"
00:11:45 - Host: "All right, that's the end of this game."
00:11:48 - Host: "Next, we'll..."

Result:
start_time: "00:05:23,000" (the rules are explained)
end_time: "00:11:45,000" (the game officially ends)
```

### Interview segment
```text
Timeline:
00:15:30 - Host: "Let's talk about your new work."
00:15:33 - Guest: "Sure, this time..."
00:22:15 - Guest: "...which is why I think it matters."
00:22:18 - Host: "Well said. Let's move to another topic."

Result:
start_time: "00:15:33,000" (the guest begins responding)
end_time: "00:22:15,000" (the discussion ends)
```

## Further checks
- Do not cut off the funniest, most suspenseful, emotional, or engaging moment.
- Do not omit necessary setup, results, audience reactions, or the natural flow of the segment.
- For variety shows, preserve fair and complete games, personality, atmosphere, pacing, and natural transitions.
- For interviews, preserve depth, authenticity, story, and flow while balancing entertainment with information.
- For performances, preserve the complete act, the performer's distinctive qualities, and audience response.
- For reality shows, preserve story, relationships, and emotional changes while balancing authenticity and drama.

## Notes
- Output strict JSON only, with no explanation.
- Use millisecond-precision `HH:MM:SS,mmm` timestamps.
- Apply `title` → `outline` and `subtopics` → `content` exactly.
- Preserve entertainment value, smooth pacing, humor, interaction, and positive presentation.
- Consider platform viewing conventions and copyright, especially for music and video.
