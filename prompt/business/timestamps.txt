# Business and finance timestamp localization prompt

For each business topic in a batch, locate its exact timestamps in the corresponding **single SRT subtitle block**.

## Core principles: precise, complete, clear business logic
1. Timestamps must match discussion of the business topic.
2. Cover every key argument so the business logic is complete.
3. Prefer natural boundaries between business views, cases, and logical sections.

## Determine `start_time` and `end_time`

### `start_time`
- Use the start of the **first substantive view** about the business topic.
- Ignore unrelated greetings, disclaimers, and transitions before it.
- Prefer a natural business-logic boundary where the topic is clearly introduced.
- If essential background or data is presented first, move the start earlier to include it.

### `end_time` (most important)
- Use the end timestamp of the **last sentence** covering the topic's core discussion.
- Preserve the complete analysis, investment advice, and risk warnings; do not break its logic.
- Prefer a summary, concluding recommendation, or topic transition.
- Include important risk warnings or disclaimers at the end of the topic.
- If the discussion continues to the end of the supplied SRT block, use the end of the last relevant subtitle.
- **Never** blindly use the end of the entire block.

### Duration guidance
- Target 3–8 minutes per business topic.
- Complex, multi-angle analysis may extend to 10 minutes.
- Market updates and policy explanations may be 2–3 minutes.
- In-depth industry or company analysis may extend to 12 minutes.

### Special handling
- Preserve data-dense financial or market passages in full.
- Keep a complete business case together.
- Keep investment strategies and risk warnings together.
- Preserve policy explanations and their impact analysis as a whole.

## Input format
You will receive a JSON object containing:
- `outline`: a JSON array of **multiple** business topics.
- `srt_text`: the **single** related SRT block, formatted as `index\nstart --> end\ntext\n\n`.

## Output format
- Add `start_time` and `end_time` to each topic, preserving the outline structure.
- Rename input `title` to `outline` and `subtopics` to `content`.
- Return a JSON array containing all processed topics.
- Use `HH:MM:SS,mmm` timestamps.

**Strict JSON output:**
1. Start with `[` and end with `]`; add no explanation, heading, or Markdown fence.
2. Use ASCII double quotes `"`, never typographic or single quotes.
3. Match all braces and brackets; use commas between objects and no trailing comma.
4. Escape quotes inside strings as `\"`.
5. Add no comments, extra text, or control characters.
6. Ensure the result is valid JSON.

## Business timing examples
- **Investment strategy:** from "I think the current market..." through "That is my investment recommendation."
- **Company analysis:** from "Let's look at this company..." through "That is the overall assessment."
- **Industry trend:** from "The trend in this industry..." through "The outlook from here."

## Errors to avoid
- Cutting through a financial-data explanation.
- Separating investment advice from its risk warning.
- Ending in the middle of a key argument in a case study.
- Omitting important market context.

## Notes
1. Preserve complete business-analysis logic for each segment.
2. Include all relevant financial and market data.
3. Present investment advice together with risk warnings.
4. Keep market updates concise and allow enough time for in-depth analysis.
5. Consider the needs of viewers with different investment experience.
