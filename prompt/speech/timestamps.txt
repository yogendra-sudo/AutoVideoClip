# Speech and stand-up video timestamp prompt

## Core principles: complete expression, emotional continuity, preserve the effect
1. Timestamps must cover a complete expressive unit or passage.
2. Each range must preserve the beginning, development, and conclusion of an emotional arc.
3. Prefer natural boundaries at topic or emotional transitions and changes in delivery.

## Choosing `start_time` and `end_time`

### `start_time`
- Use the start time of the **first substantive sentence** in the topic.
- Ignore unrelated transitions, pauses, or background music before the topic.
- Prefer the point where the topic is clearly introduced or the relevant expression begins.
- For speeches and stand-up, include important setup, introductions, or atmosphere by moving the start earlier when needed.

### `end_time` (most important)
- Use the end timestamp of the **last sentence containing the topic's core expression**.
- Include the complete argument, story, or joke; do not cut across an expressive unit.
- Prefer a summary, the conclusion after an emotional high point, or a topic transition.
- Include important audience response, laughter, or applause at the end of the topic.
- If the topic continues through the end of the supplied SRT block, use the end timestamp of its last relevant subtitle.
- **Do not** automatically use the end of the entire text block.

### Duration
- Target **3–8 minutes** per topic.
- A thoughtful or complex speech may extend to 10 minutes.
- A complete standalone joke may be 2–3 minutes.
- A central speech or important argument may extend to 12 minutes.

### Special handling
- Keep a full joke (setup, development, punchline) together.
- Preserve important emotional build-up and atmosphere.
- Keep audience interaction, questions, and live response together.
- Include the complete statement, argument, and conclusion of a central viewpoint.

## Input format
You will receive a JSON object containing:
- `outline`: a JSON array with **multiple** speech topics to process.
- `srt_text`: one SRT text block for those topics, in the format `index\nstart --> end\ntext\n\n`.

## Output format
- Add `start_time` and `end_time` to **every** topic object, preserving the input outline's structure.
- Rename the input `title` field to `outline` and `subtopics` to `content`.
- Return one JSON array containing all processed topics.
- Use timestamps in `HH:MM:SS,mmm` format.

**Strict JSON requirements:**
1. Output must begin with `[` and end with `]`. Do not add explanations, headings, or Markdown fences.
2. Use standard double quotes (`"`), never curly quotation marks or single quotes.
3. Match all braces and brackets; separate objects with commas and omit a comma after the final object.
4. Escape quotation marks inside strings as `\"`.
5. Include no comments, extra text, or control characters.
6. Ensure the output is valid JSON parseable by a standard JSON parser.

## Timestamp examples
- **Speech:** Start with the first substantive statement; end after its conclusion.
- **Stand-up:** Include the setup through the punchline and audience response.
- **Story:** Include the introduction, full story, conclusion, and reflection.
- **Live interaction:** Include the prompt to the audience through the response and summary.

Avoid cutting at the punchline, separating an opinion from its argument, truncating an emotional high point, or omitting important audience response or live effects.

## Additional considerations
- **Speeches:** Include the full argument, meaningful emotional expression, audience response, and complete insight.
- **Stand-up:** Preserve the joke's setup, development, punchline, humor, and audience laughter.
- **Interviews:** Include the complete question and answer; keep the exchange natural and preserve substantive discussion and differing views.
- **Live performance:** Preserve spontaneous performance, real-time audience interaction, performance technique, and atmosphere.

## Strategies by content type
- **Keynote:** Include the central point, supporting arguments, and conclusion; preserve emotional and educational value.
- **Stand-up:** Locate complete joke units or linked jokes; retain audience response and entertainment value.
- **Interview:** Include the question, discussion, and summary; preserve natural flow, exchange of views, and information.
- **Experience sharing:** Include context, events, and reflection; preserve the story and its lessons.

## Notes
1. Preserve the full expressive structure of each clip.
2. Include important emotion and atmosphere.
3. Retain laughter, applause, and audience response when they contribute to the moment.
4. Preserve valuable interaction and participation.
5. Include complete insight and educational value in speeches.
6. Preserve the full comedic effect in stand-up.
7. Keep the result natural and true to the live content; do not over-edit.
