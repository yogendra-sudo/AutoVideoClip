# Opinion and commentary video outline prompt

## Input format
You will receive a JSON object:
{
  "text": "{video transcript excerpt}"
}

- `text` is a human-edited transcript excerpt with coherent basic meaning.
- Use only the input. Do not add outside information, common-knowledge inferences, or assumptions.

## Core task: depth of opinion and value of critical thinking
Cover **all important opinions and arguments** while remaining concise and avoiding excessive detail. Extract **8–15 core topics**, each covering **3–8 minutes** of video.

## Topic extraction principles
- **Value of the opinion:** Prefer distinctive perspectives, original opinions, and thoughtful analysis. Include debatable opinions that invite discussion, and preserve the complete argument (claim, evidence, conclusion).
- **Depth of reasoning:** Prefer multiple perspectives and comparisons; highlight critical thinking and rational analysis; retain explicit positions and standards of judgment.
- **Social relevance:** Prefer opinions related to current social phenomena or events, broadly meaningful discussions, and material that can broaden the audience's perspective.
- **Completeness:** Every topic should state a clear position, provide a complete argument (facts, logic, and relevant emotion), and reach a clear conclusion or value judgment.

## Merge and split rules
- **Merge:** different aspects of the same social phenomenon; connected argument chains; opinions within the same value system; multiple analyses of one issue.
- **Split:** a fundamental change in position; a shift to another subject area; a shift from rational analysis to emotional expression; a significant change in standards of judgment.

## Output format
Use exactly this format and add no descriptive text:
### Outline:
1. **[Main topic]**
   - [Subtopic highlighting a distinctive, thought-provoking opinion]
   - [Subtopic]
2. **[Main topic]**
   - [Subtopic]

## Example
### Outline:
1. **How young adults' values are changing**
   - The shift from material pursuits to personal fulfillment.
   - Balancing individual interests with collective responsibility.
   - Combining traditional culture with modern ideas.
   - Conflicts between social expectations and personal choices.
2. **Critiquing online filter bubbles**
   - How recommendation algorithms shape thinking.
   - The tension between information overload and deep thought.
   - Why and how to seek diverse perspectives.
   - The importance of sound judgment amid an abundance of information.
3. **Education equity and social mobility**
   - Root causes of unequal access to educational resources.
   - Whether the idea that education changes one's prospects applies today.
   - How diverse definitions of success challenge traditional views of education.
   - Responses to the risk of entrenched social classes.

## Special considerations
- **Controversial opinions:** Present the full argument impartially, include different positions, and preserve the original context and tone.
- **Value judgments:** Distinguish facts from judgments; retain the stated position and its supporting criteria.
- **Social criticism:** Keep criticism constructive and reasoned, include analysis and possible responses, and omit empty venting.

## Notes
1. Preserve each opinion's central position and complete argument.
2. Prioritize material with depth and potential to prompt reflection.
3. Focus on distinctive, insightful perspectives on social phenomena.
4. Keep the logical chain clear.
5. Remain neutral while extracting opinions; do not judge their merits.
